What We Do

Exterior Cleaning Services in Jacksonville

One crew, one visit, every surface. We match the method to the material — high pressure where it belongs, gentle soft washing where it counts.

House Soft Washing

Vinyl, stucco, Hardie board and brick cleaned with low pressure and professional detergents that kill mold and mildew at the root instead of just blasting it off. No streaks, no etched siding, no water forced behind your walls.

  • Siding, soffits & fascia
  • Window exteriors & screens rinsed
  • Spider webs & wasp nests removed

Roof Soft Washing

Those black streaks are gloeocapsa magma — a living algae that feeds on limestone filler and shortens shingle life. We treat it with the low-pressure method recommended by the Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association, so granules stay put and your warranty stays valid.

  • Asphalt shingle, tile & metal roofs
  • Kills algae, moss & lichen at the root
  • Zero high-pressure contact

Driveway & Sidewalk Washing

Surface-cleaner equipment delivers an even, stripe-free finish across concrete — not the zebra stripes a wand leaves behind. Rust, oil drips, irrigation stains and tire marks get targeted pre-treatment before the main clean.

  • Driveways, walkways & entryways
  • Oil, rust & battery-acid spot treatment
  • Curb & garage apron included

Paver, Patio & Pool Deck Restoration

Pavers, travertine and stamped concrete cleaned at a pressure the material can actually take. We can re-sand joints and apply a breathable sealer that locks in color and slows the next round of weed and algae growth.

  • Optional joint re-sanding & sealing
  • Slip-resistant, pool-safe finish
  • Screen enclosures & lanais

Fence & Deck Cleaning

Gray, weathered wood is usually surface mildew, not permanent damage. Controlled-pressure cleaning brings the natural grain back and preps the wood properly if you plan to stain or seal it afterward.

  • Decks, docks & pergolas

FAQ

Questions We Hear Every Week

How often should I have my Jacksonville home washed?

Most homes here need a soft wash every 12 to 18 months. Jacksonville's humidity, heavy summer rain and coastal salt air grow mold, mildew and algae far faster than drier climates. Homes under heavy tree cover or on the north side of a lot often need annual service.

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water and is the right call for concrete, brick and pavers. Soft washing uses low pressure combined with professional cleaning solutions that kill organic growth at the root — the only safe method for roofs, siding, stucco, screens and painted surfaces. We use both, and we choose based on the material, not on what is faster.

Will soft washing damage my roof shingles?

No. Soft washing is the method recommended by the Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association precisely because it avoids high pressure. Granules stay in place, seals stay intact, and your shingle warranty is unaffected. Pressure washing a shingle roof, by contrast, can strip years off its life in a single afternoon.

Are your cleaning solutions safe for pets, plants and landscaping?

Yes. We pre-wet all surrounding landscaping, use biodegradable detergents, tarp especially delicate plantings, and rinse thoroughly when we finish. Pets and children can be back outside as soon as the surfaces are dry, typically within an hour.

Do I need to be home during the service?

Not at all. We need access to an outdoor water spigot and to the areas being cleaned. Plenty of our customers are at work during service — we text when we arrive, text when we finish, and send before-and-after photos so you can see exactly what was done.

How much does pressure washing cost in Jacksonville?

It depends on square footage, surface type and how much organic growth has built up. A typical single-story house wash starts around $249, standard driveways around $149, and bundled house-plus-driveway packages save roughly 15%. You always get a flat written quote before we schedule — never an hourly rate that grows while you watch.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Renew Exterior Cleaning is licensed in Duval County and carries both general liability and workers' compensation coverage. We are glad to send a certificate of insurance before any work begins — and you should ask every contractor for one.

What if I am not happy with the results?

Tell us and we come back. Our spotless-finish guarantee means any missed area gets re-cleaned at no charge, no debate and no additional invoice. We would rather spend another hour on your property than have you tell a neighbor we cut corners.
